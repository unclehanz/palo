How to play

  • You control Brgy. Mapayapa (white, near side). Every touch your team makes is yours: serve, pass, set, spike, block.
  • Swipe controls (default). A pulsing circle grows and shrinks on the court: release your drag when it is smallest for PERFECT. Drag direction and length = where the ball goes. Set: drag onto a teammate's zone (middle zone then over the net = quick). Spike / serve: drag to the spot you want to hit. Short drag forward = tip. Settings → Controls switches back to Classic.
  • Classic · right thumb · one button. A circle shrinks under the player who's about to touch the ball: red → orange → green. Press on green for PERFECT. Better ratings give a wider green.
  • Left thumb · stick. Serve and spike: left / right aims, up = tip. Set: left = outside hitter, right = opposite, down = middle quick, up = setter dump (or back-row pipe).
  • Pressing deep in the red can cause a fault: serve into the net or out, double contact, foot fault, net touch. Not pressing at all lets the ball drop.
  • 6v6 with real rotation: you rotate on side-out, the server is whoever is in zone 1, and the libero (Ica) swaps in for the back-row middle.
  • Perfect touches fill HYPE. When it's full, your next spike is a SUPER PALO.
  • Keyboard: arrows / WASD = stick, Space / J = button, Esc = pause.
